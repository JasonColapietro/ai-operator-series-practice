# Practice files: The AI Operator Series

Companion files for *First Login*, *Working With Claude*, *Build With Agents*, *Own Your Work* and *First Client* by Jason Colapietro.

Every "Try this" exercise in the five books works with your own material or with the files here.

**The people and businesses in this folder are fiction.** Harbor & Home Cleaning, Dana Ruiz, Sam Rivera and Marcus Lee Design are invented, and so are their emails, phone numbers, prices and customers. The prices exist to be checked, not copied.

Two kinds of file need a second look:

- **Example results** (`*-example-result.txt`) are real output from one run in Claude, kept unchanged so you can compare shape, not words. Your run will differ. Where one repeats a fact from the fictional notes, the notes file wins.
- **Research examples** name real places, agencies and rules as a search found them on the date in the file's header. They show what a sourced answer looks like. They are not a source: open every link and check it yourself before you rely on anything in them.

These files turn on no plugin, hook or setting in any tool you open them with.

Layout:

- `shared/`: files the whole series uses, starting with the Harbor & Home facts and voice sample. They come from the *Claude AI Made Practical* practice pack, unchanged.
- `b1/` to `b5/`: one folder per book, then one per chapter (`b1/ch05/`), holding only what that chapter's exercises need.

## Get the files

Choose the green **Code** button above, then **Download ZIP**, and unzip it anywhere you like. Each chapter's folder has a `README.txt` that lists its files and the exercise they go with.

The books: [suedeai.ai/books](https://suedeai.ai/books). Corrections for all five books are listed there too.
