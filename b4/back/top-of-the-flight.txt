  identity.md records one name for Sam's work, and the plugin's
  author line now says "Sam Rivera" in place of a placeholder.
  The night job's agent step was replaced by a plain Python script,
  tested on two computers.

  The checker's line split in two. The method (code, tests and the
  briefs) sits under Assets as Sam's, with Harbor & Home's license
  noted. Harbor & Home's running copy sits under Held for others as

  letter gives Dana three lines: her Monday report is hers to keep
