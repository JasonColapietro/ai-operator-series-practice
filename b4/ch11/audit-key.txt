PRACTICE FILE, FICTION. Book 4, Chapter 11. What the handle audit and the markup step accept.
Read with harbor-home-profiles.txt. Every name and address here is invented.

THE AUDIT (Try this, steps 1 and 2)
Accept any table that flags these three, in any order and any wording:
  - Invoice template: "Harbor and Home Cleaning Co." does not match. Fix: use the chosen name and add the website.
  - Review-site listing: "Harbor Home Cleaners" does not match and has no link. Fix: claim the listing or ask the
    site to correct the name, and fill the Website field.
  - Dana's networking profile: right person, but it never names the business or links the About page.
    Fix: name the business in the headline or job and link the About page.
Also accept:
  - Flagging the website footer as "none, this is the name to match".
  - Marking the social page and the directory as fine. Both match and link back.
  - Treating the invoice as a "place the name appears" rather than a profile. It isn't a profile, and it still counts,
    because customers read it and search for the name on it.
  - Choosing "Harbor & Home Cleaning" as the one name. A reader who argues for another spelling is fine, as long as
    every row is then measured against that one spelling.
Don't accept: a table that passes the review listing because "it's obviously them". A stranger, or a machine,
can't tell that from the page.

THE MARKUP (Try this, step 4)
Accept any JSON-LD that passes the Schema Markup Validator with no errors and:
  - names the business "Harbor & Home Cleaning" and Dana as "Dana Ruiz";
  - lists the social page and the directory under the business's sameAs, and, if the reader also wrote a Person block
    for Dana, her networking profile under hers. An Organization-only block that leaves Dana's profile out is fine.
    Don't accept Dana's profile in the business's sameAs: sameAs means "this same thing", and Dana isn't the business;
  - leaves the review listing OUT of sameAs until it shows the chosen name;
  - claims nothing the About page doesn't show. That includes the profile links: the About page links none today,
    so a "Not on the page" list that names them is correct, and the fix is to add them to the page (step 3).
    The 4.8 rating and the 52 reviews are on the review site,
    not on the About page, so any aggregateRating or review property is wrong here.
harbor-home-about-markup.html is one version that passes (validated 2026-11-07: 0 errors, 0 warnings). An Organization-only
block, or a Person block with worksFor, also passes.

the review listing waits on the site, so "requested, waiting" counts as done for the exercise.
