SAM'S DEPOSIT CHECKER, AT THE END OF BOOK 3

What it is
  scripts/reconcile.py, a short Python script. It matches each of
  Harbor & Home's bank deposits to the invoice it pays and lists every
  deposit and every invoice that doesn't match. A second script,
  scripts/monday_report.py, turns what it prints into Dana's Monday
  report.

Who made it
  Sam, in Book 3, working with coding agents and reading each change
  before committing it. Dana made the calls on the hard cases, such
  as a transfer that pays two invoices.

Where it lives
  In Sam's library repository: a folder on Sam's laptop, pushed to a
  private repository on GitHub under Sam's own account.

What it reads
  Harbor & Home's weekly bank exports and invoice lists, committed in
  the same repository under practice/harbor-home/. In the story these
  are Dana's real business records, with a customer's name on every
  payment.

The tools behind it
  The coding agents run on Sam's own accounts: Claude Pro on Sam's
  Claude account, and ChatGPT Plus for Codex. Harbor & Home pays for
  both plans as a business cost. The script itself is plain Python and
  runs without either agent.

What is written down about it
  The repository's history: every commit, with its date and a message
  saying what changed. Handoff notes between sessions, incidents.md
  (what went wrong) and decisions.md (what was decided).

What is not written down anywhere
  Who owns the code, and on what terms anyone else may use it.
  Which lines an agent wrote and which Sam wrote.
