## Sovereignty score (scored YYYY-MM-DD)

Score each ability 0, 1 or 2.
- 0: it depends on one vendor or one person. Someone else has to agree, or it stops when they do.
- 1: you could do it, with hours of work or a step someone has to remember.
- 2: you can do it today, without asking anyone.

Prove: can you show it's yours, and since when, from records you hold?
Terms: are the terms for its use written by you (or its owner), not set by a vendor's screen?
Transfer: could you hand it on, or move it to another tool or machine, today?
