VOICE AND LIKENESS POLICY: HARBOR & HOME CLEANING
Owner: Dana Ruiz
Kept by: Sam Rivera
Updated: August 19, 2027
Next review: January 2028, or the day anything new speaks for the business

1. WHERE OUR VOICES AND FACES ARE
- Dana's greeting on the office line, recorded by Dana.
- Team photos on the business page: the holiday team photo and the meet-the-team posts for Sarah and Miguel.
- voice.md, the written description of how Dana writes (library/projects/harbor-home/voice.md).
- We don't record calls.

2. WHAT MAY USE THEM, AND FOR WHAT
- Claude, in the Harbor & Home Project and the harbor-reply skill, drafts customer emails from voice.md. Dana reads every draft and sends it herself.
- No tool clones Dana's voice, or anyone's voice or face, and no tool makes new images or audio of anyone on the team.

3. NEW USES
- Dana approves any new use of a voice, face or name, in writing, before it happens.
- Every yes gets a record in consent/, named with the date first.
- Editing a photo or recording with AI, or making anything new from it, needs its own yes.
- When someone leaves, Dana asks whether their photo may stay. Without a written yes, it comes down. (Open: the holiday photo with Dev. Dana asks this week.)

4. MONEY
- A new payee, or new bank details for anyone, at any amount: Sam calls Dana on the number already saved in Sam's phone, and pays only after Dana confirms on that call.
- Voicemail, text or email never authorizes a payment on its own, however much it sounds like Dana.
- Nobody calls a number given in the request.
- Dana never asks for a payment by voicemail. A voicemail that does is not from Dana.

5. ANYTHING THAT SPEAKS OR SENDS FOR US
- Today nothing does. Claude drafts; Dana sends. The Monday summary goes to Dana only.
- Before any assistant answers the office line or the inbox in the business's name, it has to answer the four questions:
  1. Proof: every call or message leaves a record Dana can open, with the time and a transcript or the sent text.
  2. Silence: a daily count of what it handled, checked against the phone bill and the inbox. A count it couldn't take is reported as "unknown", never as zero.
  3. Identity: it speaks only on the business line and inbox, and says it is Harbor & Home's assistant (our disclosure line).
  4. Unclear outcomes: it never quotes a price outside the facts file, books a job, charges anyone or resends anything on its own. Anything unclear goes on Dana's morning list.
- A tool that can't answer one of these doesn't speak for us.

6. IF SOMEONE FAKES US
- Keep the message, voicemail or post. Don't reply, and don't call any number in it.
- Tell Dana the same day. Dana decides whether to warn customers and where to report it.
- Sam writes it down with the date.
