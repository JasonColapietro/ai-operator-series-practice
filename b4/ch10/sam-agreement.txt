BOOKKEEPING SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Harbor & Home Cleaning ("Harbor & Home"), owner Dana Ruiz, and Sam Rivera ("Sam").

1. Work. Sam will keep Harbor & Home's books part time: enter and match deposits and invoices, prepare the weekly numbers and the Monday summary, and help with other office tasks Dana asks for.

2. Hours and pay. Sam will log hours each week and send Dana a bill every two weeks. Harbor & Home will pay each bill within 15 days at the hourly rate the two of them agreed in writing.

3. Tools. Sam will use Sam's own computer and accounts. Harbor & Home will pay for the AI plans Sam uses for this work as a business cost.

4. Work product. All work product belongs to Harbor & Home.

5. Confidentiality. Sam will keep customer names, addresses, payment details and Harbor & Home's financial information private and will use them only for this work.

6. Ending. Either side may end this agreement with two weeks' notice by email.

7. Independent contractor. Sam is an independent contractor, not an employee, and is responsible for Sam's own taxes.

Signed: Dana Ruiz, for Harbor & Home Cleaning
Signed: Sam Rivera
