## Model clauses for AI services [attorney review before reuse]

1. **Your data, accounts and keys.** Your data, content, accounts, passwords and keys stay yours. I use them only for this work, and I return or delete my copies within [DAYS] days after the work ends. [attorney review before reuse]
2. **My pre-existing materials.** My library, skills, prompts, rules files and plugins, including anything I made before this work, stay mine. You get a nonexclusive license to use them on your accounts for as long as you use the deliverables they're part of. [attorney review before reuse]
3. **What you pay for.** The deliverables listed in [SECTION] become yours by written assignment when you've paid the invoice for them in full. [attorney review before reuse]
4. **AI use.** I use AI tools in this work. I name each tool and keep a dated record of its terms, and you can see both. [attorney review before reuse]
5. **Your approval.** No agent sends, publishes, books or charges anything in your name without your written approval of that action. [attorney review before reuse]

## Questions for a lawyer

- When does ownership pass under this contract?
- What do I keep from my pre-existing materials?
- Am I covering claims about content I did not create?
- Does the liability cap cover the indemnity, or is the indemnity carved out of it?
- Which state's law governs this agreement, and where would a dispute be heard?
