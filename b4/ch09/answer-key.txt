Accept all of these ways of getting there:
  - Listing 9.2 with the Google-Extended line removed (harbor-home-robots-final.txt).
  - Any order of groups. Crawlers pick the group that names them, wherever it sits.
  - The search crawlers left out entirely, with no "User-agent: *" group or with one that allows
    everything. They come out allowed either way; naming them just makes the intent readable.
  - A "User-agent: *" group with "Allow: /" or an empty "Disallow:".
  - The __usage_ai-training group present or absent. It blocks no crawler, so no status changes.
    Present is closer to Dana's training answer.
  - A Sitemap line present or absent.
Reject:
  - Any "User-agent: *" group with "Disallow: /" (Claude-User, ChatGPT-User, Perplexity-User and
    unnamed bots come out blocked: Sam's first fix).
  - Google-Extended blocked without a note that Dana changed her mind.
  - A status of "allowed" for any bot when the live file couldn't be read. That one is "unverified".
    The exception: an address that answers "not found" (HTTP 404 or 410) means the site has no
    robots.txt, and every bot is "allowed", with a note saying the file is missing.

Status words: allowed, blocked, unverified. "Allowed" and "blocked" describe what the file asks.
They don't show what a crawler does.
