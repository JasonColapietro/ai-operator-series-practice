PRACTICE FILE. The AI Operator Series, Book 2, Chapter 6.
Four questions to answer before a contract, a health record or a money document goes into Claude, and the three answers you can reach. The same card the chapter prints. It isn't legal advice.
----------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU UPLOAD
1. Who else is in it? Names, addresses, health, money. Their details are partly their decision.
2. Does this account train on it? Read the training setting in Settings > Privacy and decide on purpose.
3. How long will it be kept, and where? The chat, any memory made from it, and any file you added to a Project are three places, and each one is deleted separately.
4. Is this account the right home? A personal account runs under personal terms, whoever the work is for. Patient information needs a plan sold with a Business Associate Agreement.

THREE ANSWERS
- Upload it as it is.
- Trim it first: replace what the job doesn't need with a placeholder in capitals and square brackets, such as [ACCOUNT NUMBER].
- Keep it out of this account, or move the job to an account that fits.

TWO QUESTIONS TO ASK
- The person or business the document belongs to: may this go into an AI tool, and what should come out first?
- A lawyer, your employer or the plan's admin: does an agreement or a rule in your field limit where this information can go?
