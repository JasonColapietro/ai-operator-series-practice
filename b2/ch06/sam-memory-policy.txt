CLAUDE MAY KEEP

CLAUDE MAY NOT KEEP (and where it goes instead)
- Anything on my never-paste list. It goes nowhere. If one turns up in memory, I change it that day and delete the topic and the chat it came from.
- Totals, balances and who still owes. They live in that week's summary in library/, and I paste them fresh.
- Prices, hours and policies. They live in facts.md. A price Claude picked up from an example gets deleted on sight.
- Customers' private news, family or health. The booking app holds what the job needs, and nothing else holds any of it.
- My own money and housing. I ask about those in a chat with memory off.
- Rules nobody decided. If a topic says how Dana does something, I ask Dana. Her answer goes in brief.md, in her words, and the topic goes.
