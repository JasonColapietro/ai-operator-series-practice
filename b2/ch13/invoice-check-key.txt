THE ACCEPTANCE LINES

All five pass on this data, and the reply marks each one pass or fail:
1. No invoice number in two rows: INV-31188 is one row with both file names.
2. No order matched twice: four rows, four orders, one each.
3. Every line checked: the only line that differs is Pine State's paper towels, and it is on the Exceptions list with both quantities.
4. NGC-1031 matched by items to PO-0112, and marked.
5. The invoice column, $1,008.25, against the order log's $975.13: the $33.12 between them is the Pine State line on the Exceptions list.
