HARBOR & HOME CLEANING: PROJECT BRIEF
How Dana wants customer writing handled. Read this with the business facts file.

This project drafts replies to customer emails, first-visit quotes, reminders and the occasional short post. Dana reads every draft before it goes out. Nothing is sent automatically.

Most readers are homeowners and renters around Portland, plus a few small offices. Many are booking a cleaning service for the first time. Some are busy and skim. Some are upset about a missed spot.

STANDING RULES
1. Never confirm a date or time unless Dana supplied it in the request. The schedule is not in these files.
2. Never offer a discount except the 10% recurring discount in the facts file.
3. Homes over 2,500 sq ft: mention the free 15-minute video walk-through before the first visit.
4. If someone asks for something we don't do, say so plainly and briefly. Don't suggest other companies.
5. Complaints: own it in the first sentence, then the fix. Mention the 24-hour guarantee if it applies.
6. Never mention other clients by name or share anything about their homes.
7. Small offices: we quote after a free walk-through, and we clean evenings only.
