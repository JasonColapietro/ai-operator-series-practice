Q1. Price and windows

- Also right: a figure worked out with the size step, if the reply says it borrowed that step from the Standard home clean line and flags it. The facts print the step on the standard clean line only, so they don't settle whether it applies to a deep clean. A flagged "from $285" (the deep-clean base plus one size step) passes, and so does a flagged figure that adds the travel fee.
