PRACTICE FILE, FICTION. The AI Operator Series, Book 2, Chapter 12.
What a good set of follow-ups to quiet-quotes.txt gets right, for "Try this: Run the top item". Written for this book. A follow-up is not a fixed result, so this key lists what each draft must carry and what it must leave out. It names the facts-file section behind a point instead of repeating prices. Everyone named is invented.
----------------------------------------
EVERY DRAFT
- A subject line, under 120 words, signed "Dana", no exclamation points.
- Opens with the useful part. No "just checking in," "following up," "I haven't heard back" or a reminder that they haven't replied.
- Ends with one question the customer can answer in a line.
- No price, discount or date anywhere, including prices Dana already sent. If a draft needs one, it carries a [CHECK: ...] (or CHECK:, the Project's spelling, which is the same marker).
- A list under it of things to sort out before sending.

THREAD 1: ELLIOT HOLLOWAY
Must carry: Sarah's answer on the nervous dog, from Dana's notes: the dog in one room with the door shut, that room done last, and the dog let out into a finished room first.
Must leave out: any start date. The week of February 8 is his question, and the schedule isn't in any file (project brief, rule 1). Restating the price or the recurring discount.
A good closing question: whether that would work for his dog, or whether he'd still like Dana to check the start week he asked about (without naming the date, which the Check rules out).
The sort-out list should catch: the schedule for the week of February 8 still hasn't been checked; pet-safe products are on request (facts, SUPPLIES), so ask whether he wants them.

THREAD 2: MAYA SORENSEN
Must carry: yes, the move-out clean covers inside the oven and the baseboards (Dana's notes, from Sarah); and an invitation to send the landlord's checklist ahead, so the team can work from it.
Must leave out: any deal for booking both. The only discount is the recurring one (facts, SERVICES AND PRICES), and Dana already told her. The spring renters' package (facts, NEW IN SPRING): it carries a price and doesn't launch until March 1, after her move. Any yes to the carpets (facts, WHAT WE DO NOT DO). Confirming the end of the month or any date.
A good closing question: whether she'd like to send the checklist over.
The sort-out list should catch: the move-out clean's oven and baseboards aren't in the facts file yet, so add them there first; her move-out date isn't booked; the carpet line on her landlord's list is still hers to arrange.

THREAD 3: DANIEL KWAN
Must carry: what a deep clean covers, from the deep clean line in the facts file (inside the oven, the baseboards, the interior windows); the free 15-minute video walk-through for homes over 2,500 square feet (facts, HOW BOOKING WORKS).
Must leave out: any price for 2,700 square feet. The facts file publishes a deep clean only up to 1,500 square feet, and its size step sits on the standard clean line, so a figure here is worked out, not approved. Any promise to finish before his parents arrive, or any February date.
A good closing question: whether a video walk-through would suit him or Teresa, or whether Dana should send this straight to Teresa.
The sort-out list should catch: Teresa isn't on the thread and Dana doesn't have her email; the parents' dates are only "early February"; three lines may be thin for someone who wants to see what's included, which is a reason to add more to facts.md.

A RESULT THAT FAILS
- Any figure for Daniel's house, however it was worked out.
- A start date for Elliot, or "we'd be happy to start the week of February 8."
- A bundle or loyalty discount for Maya.
- A draft that opens by mentioning the earlier quote or the silence.
Send one repair, in the same chat: "Check each draft against the Check part of my request. Take out every price, discount and date, put [CHECK: what is missing] where one was needed, and start each email with the thing I promised."

REVIEW TIME
Reading three drafts against this key, then against your own sense of each customer, should take under ten minutes. Longer than that means the job is too wide or a note is missing. Narrow it or fix the notes before you run it again.
