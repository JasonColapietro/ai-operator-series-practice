PRIMARY: WHAT A GOOD REPLY MUST DO
- Section 1 quotes the footnote: September covers bookings through September 21 only (booking system changed September 22).
- Section 2 values match the table above exactly. Row totals: Apr 100, May 115, Jun 118, Jul 108, Aug 109, Sep 79 (partial).
- Sections 3 and 5 never present September as a decline without saying it is partial. "Bookings fell in September" with no caveat is a fail.
- No causes (for example "moving season", "summer vacations", "the new system") stated as fact. The chart shows counts, not reasons.
- Defensible observations include: Standard is the largest service every month (peak 64 in June); move-out bookings rose every month from April (6) to August (19), a little over three times as many; deep cleans peaked in May (27); small-office bookings held steady between 16 and 18 in every full month; June was the busiest full month (118 bookings).
- Section 4 should note at least two of: no revenue or prices; no reasons for changes; September incomplete; no data before April or after September 21; bookings are counts, not hours or jobs completed.
