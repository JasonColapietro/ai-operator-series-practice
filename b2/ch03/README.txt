PRACTICE FILES, FICTION. The AI Operator Series, Book 2, Chapter 3, Numbers You Can Trust.
Everything named in these files is invented: Harbor & Home Cleaning, Dana Ruiz, Sam Rivera, the crews, every client and business, and every address, phone number, email, price and booking count. The prices exist so you can check a summary against them, not to quote.
Files marked "copied unchanged" come from the Claude AI Made Practical practice pack. Text files carry a short header and a line of dashes above the pack's words. The two CSV files and the chart image carry no header, because a header row would change the row counts and the picture the lessons check; this README is their fiction label.
