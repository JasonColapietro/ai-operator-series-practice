PRACTICE FILES, FICTION. The AI Operator Series, Book 2, Chapter 11, Your First Skill.
Everything named in these files is invented: Harbor & Home Cleaning, Dana Ruiz, Sam Rivera, the crews, every client, and every price, booking and job ID. The towns are real places in and around Portland, Maine.
The two files inside the weekly-numbers folder carry no practice-file header above their first line, because a skill's SKILL.md has to open with its frontmatter. This README is their fiction label, and columns.md also says so under its title.

For "Try this: Package your most-used keeper"
skill-request.txt                 The request that asks Claude to draft a skill from a keeper, with slots for your own job. Matches the chapter's card word for word below its header.
skill-request-harbor-home.txt     The same request with Harbor & Home's values filled in and the weekly-summary card from chapter 3 pasted in its place. The practice route starts here.
weekly-numbers/SKILL.md           The weekly-numbers skill after Sam's edits. Matches Listing 11.1 in the chapter word for word. Compare your draft with it.
weekly-numbers/columns.md         The skill's reference file: what each column in the jobs export means. Claude opens it only when a column is unclear.
weekly-numbers.zip                The weekly-numbers folder, zipped with the folder at the top, ready to upload if your own ZIP keeps failing. Make your own first; zipping it is part of the lesson.
trigger-tests.txt                 The three requests for the trigger test (one that should load the skill, a near miss and an unrelated one), with what each should do.

Also used, from elsewhere in the practice files:
practice/b2/ch03/harbor-home-jobs-oct-12-16.csv   The week of jobs the first trigger test summarizes.
practice/b2/ch03/weekly-summary-key.txt           The numbers a correct summary of that week has to get right.
practice/shared/harbor-home-business-facts.txt    The price list. The skill reads it; it never copies a price into itself.

Nothing here turns on a plugin, hook or setting in any tool you open it with. The skill does nothing until you upload it to your own Claude account and switch it on.
