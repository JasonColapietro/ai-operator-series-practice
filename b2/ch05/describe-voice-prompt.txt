I'm writing a one-page description of how I write, so that drafts written for me sound like me. Below are [NUMBER] messages I wrote and sent.

Describe the patterns you see. Don't write anything in my voice yet.

Use five headings: Perspective, Structure, Vocabulary, Themes, Never.
- Perspective: where I stand when I write, and what I seem to believe about the reader.
- Structure: how each message is built, from the first line to the last.
- Vocabulary: words and phrases I reach for, how long my sentences run, how I greet and sign off.
- Themes: what I keep coming back to.
- Never: things most writers do that I never do in these messages.

Under each pattern, quote one sentence from my messages that shows it. If a pattern shows up in only one message, say so.

Describe how I write, never what I wrote about. Leave every price, date, time and name out of the description.
