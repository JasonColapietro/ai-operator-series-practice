PRACTICE FILE. The AI Operator Series, Book 2, Chapter 5.
The blind test on one card, plus a blank entry for library/reviews/blind-tests.md. It works for your own messages or the practice route. Practice-route names are invented.
----------------------------------------
GET THREE MESSAGES READY
1. One draft from Claude, written in your Project as a reply to a real message.
2. Two messages you wrote and sent, of the same kind: two replies to customers, or two notes to your team.
3. In the draft, fill in every [CHECK] with any answer that reads naturally, and delete the list under it. This copy is for reading, never for sending.
4. In all three, take out other people's last names, addresses, phone numbers and anything private. Use the same greeting form and the same sign-off in each.
5. Paste each one as plain text on its own page, with no dates or email headers. Number them 1 to 3 in shuffled order, and write down where your reader can't see it which number is the draft.

WHAT TO SAY
"One of these three was drafted by an AI. I wrote the other two. Which one is the AI's, and what gave it away?"

AFTERWARD
- Picked, with a reason: the reason is your tell. Add it to voice.md under the heading it belongs to, replace the copy in your Project, and run a new round with a new draft and new samples.
- Picked, with no reason ("it just felt off"): ask which sentence. If they can't point to one, count it as a guess.
- Not picked: a pass. A pure guess still misses the draft two times in three, so one pass is a start, and two in a row, with different readers, are worth trusting.
