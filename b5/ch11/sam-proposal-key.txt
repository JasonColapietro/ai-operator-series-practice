THE FOUR PLANTED PROBLEMS

1. A price that is not on the sheet. "Facts file cleanup | $250". Sam's sheet has no such line. Its nearest step is the facts audit at $350, which is a separate offer. Everything that follows from the $250 is wrong too: the $1,500 total, the $750 deposit and the $4,800 to $6,450 first-year range.
   Repair: delete the line. Checking the twenty answers with Dana is part of the $1,250 package ("one inbox, twenty questions, thirty days"). Total $1,250; deposit $625; first year $1,250 + 11 x $300 = $4,550 to $1,250 + 11 x $450 = $6,200.

2. A fixed date. "The agent goes live on Monday, June 12". Sam controls none of what that date depends on.
   Repair: a range, with its dependencies under it. Start Monday, June 12 to Monday, June 19, 2028. Decision review Friday, July 14 to Friday, July 21. These dates depend on the signed page and deposit by Wednesday, June 7; inbox access set up in week 1; the twenty answers checked by the Friday of week 1. A late item moves every date by the same amount.

3. A promised result. "you'll never lose a weekend job to a faster company again". The pilot drafts; Dana sends; nobody controls who answers a customer first.
   Repair: delete the sentence. The number the pilot moves (median time to first reply) belongs in the statement of work, as a measurement, with no target.

4. Assumptions in a paragraph. In section 2: "I'm assuming the facts file is current and that you'll keep handling phone messages yourself."
   Repair: move both into section 5 as their own numbered lines: "4. Your facts file is current, and you keep it current." and "5. Phone messages, weekday quotes, the FAQ page and the Monday summary are not in this pilot."
