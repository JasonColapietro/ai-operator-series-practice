# Discovery call
Last updated: [DATE]

## 1. The day before
Run pre-call-audit-template.md (Listing 9.1) on them and on me. Fix or name every FAIL on my side.
Send the screenshot or scorecard, with three lines on how the twenty minutes will go.

## 2. The twenty minutes
0 to 5: what the engines said. They read the screenshot aloud. One question: is that who you lose to?
5 to 15: their week. The seven questions below. They talk; I type their words in quotation marks.
15 to 20: one next step. Read back one job and one number. A draft scope by tomorrow, or the honest answer: a short fix their own team can run.
No slides. Record only after a clear yes at the start. Never take a password, a code or a key.

## 3. The seven questions
1. Where did last week go?
2. What happens to a quote request that arrives at 9 p.m.?
3. Which question do you or your staff answer every day?
4. What did you last lose, and why?
5. Which tools and accounts would this touch, and whose name is each one in?
6. Who would own it after launch?
7. What one number would tell you it worked?
Mark each job they name: P (someone else reads or hears it), $ (money), U (can't be taken back). All three: split it. The agent drafts; the owner decides and sends.

## 4. My answers to a buyer's twelve questions
The work
1. Which process first, and what does done look like?
2. What runs alone, and what waits for a person? (in writing)
3. Who owns it after launch?
4. What one number does it report to?
Where we work
5. Have I run this for a business like theirs, with their busy and slow seasons?
6. Which local rules apply (recording consent, advertising rules for their trade)?
7. Who will they talk to, and where am I?
Data and the model
8. Where does their data go, is any of it used for training, and how long is it kept?
9. What happens when the model is wrong?
10. What is it built on, and can they leave with it?
Money
11. How is it priced, and what makes the bill go up?
12. What are the first ninety days, and what can they cancel?
