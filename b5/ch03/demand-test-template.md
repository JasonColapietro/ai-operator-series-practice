## Rules, applied in this order

1. Over the envelope on days, hours or money: **stop the test**, whatever the interest.
2. Fewer than [NUMBER] complete conversations when the calendar runs out: **pause**. An incomplete sample is never a failure.
3. Fewer than [NUMBER] could say the offer back in their own words: **revise the offer**.
4. Nobody asked for a scope: **stop this version** of the offer.
5. At least [NUMBER] understood and at least [NUMBER] asked for a scope: **continue** with one more bounded test.
6. Anything else: **pause** on mixed evidence.

How to count:

- A conversation counts when a matching buyer answers all seven questions on the script.
- **Understood** means they said back, in their own words, what they would get and what they would supply.
- **Asked for scope** means a yes to question 6. Praise is not a yes.
- **Paid or committed** needs a payment or a signed scope. "I'd pay for that" is a note, not a commitment.
