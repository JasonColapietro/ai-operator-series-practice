PRACTICE FILES, FICTION. The AI Operator Series, Book 5, Chapter 3, Test Demand Before You Build.
Everything named in these files is invented: Harbor & Home Cleaning, Dana Ruiz, Sam Rivera, the Front Office Kit, Lise Tremblay, Meera Nair, Hank Doucette, Marta Costa, Roland Bissonnette and their companies. Prices for Harbor & Home live in one place, practice/shared/harbor-home-business-facts.txt; nothing here repeats them. No Suede AI price appears anywhere in these files.

For "Try this: Rules before results"
demand-test-template.md       The blank demand test: envelope, rules, register, envelope used and decision. Copy it to library/offers/<your-offer>/demand-test.md and fill the envelope and the rules before you contact anyone.

For "Try this: A one-week demand test"
buyer-script.txt              The seven-question buyer conversation, the same card the chapter prints. Print it or keep it open on the call.
sam-buyer-notes.txt           Sam's typed notes from the test week: two entries from before the test, then five conversations. Fill the register from these if you are not running your own test.
sam-demand-test-key.md        Sam's finished demand test, with the register and the decision. Compare yours with it after you finish, not before.

The notes use test days (Day 1 to Day 5) instead of calendar dates. Your own file should carry real dates, because the date on the rules is what proves they came first.

These files turn on no plugin, hook or setting in any tool you open them with.
