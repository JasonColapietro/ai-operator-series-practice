PRACTICE FILE. The AI Operator Series, Book 5, Chapter 3. The buyer conversation script, the same card the chapter prints.
------------------------------------------------------------
BUYER CONVERSATION. 20 minutes. Do not pitch.
Open: "I'm testing an idea, and I won't sell you anything today.
I'd like to hear how this works in your business now."

1. Tell me about the last time a customer's question cost you
   a job or an evening. What happened?
2. Where do your answers and prices live today, and who keeps
   them up to date?
   [Show the one finished sample. Stay quiet for a minute.]
3. In your own words, what would this give you, and what would
   you have to hand over?
4. Which part would you use? Which would you skip? What's unclear?
5. If you bought something like this, who would say yes, and who
   would supply the facts?
6. Would looking at a specific scope for your business be a
   useful next step?
7. What would you need to know before we talk about price?

Close: thank them, say when you'll follow up, and type your notes
before the next call.
