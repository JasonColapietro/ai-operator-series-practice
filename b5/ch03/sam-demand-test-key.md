# Demand test: Front Office Kit, version 1

Written: Day 1, Monday, before 9 a.m., before the first message. The envelope and the rules below do not change after this. A better rule goes in the next test.

## Envelope

- **Buyers:** 5 owners who would pay for and approve the kit, at home-cleaning companies with five to fifteen staff, within an hour's drive of Portland, Maine. Not Dana: she already has the build, and I work for her. Not anyone's staff.
- **Calendar:** 7 days, Day 1 (Monday) to Day 7 (Sunday)
- **Active hours:** 6, counting outreach, conversations, driving and notes
- **Money:** $40, for gas, coffee and printing
- **Sample shown:** the Harbor & Home FAQ as Dana published it, with her written yes. The Monday summary is described in words only, because it lists Dana's customers and invoices.

## Rules, applied in this order

1. Over the envelope on days, hours or money: **stop the test**, whatever the interest.
2. Fewer than 5 complete conversations when the calendar runs out: **pause**. An incomplete sample is never a failure.
3. Fewer than 4 could say the kit back in their own words: **revise the offer**.
4. Nobody asked for a scope: **stop this version** of the offer.
5. At least 4 understood and at least 2 asked for a scope: **continue** with one more bounded test.
6. Anything else: **pause** on mixed evidence.

How to count: a conversation counts when a matching owner answers all seven questions. Understood means they said back what they would get and what they would supply. Asked for scope means a yes to question 6. Paid or committed needs a payment or a signed scope.

## Decision

- **Date:** Day 5, Friday, 6 p.m.
- **Counts:** understood 4 of 5, asked for scope 2 of 5, paid or committed 0 of 5
- **Rule that applied:** rule 5. Continue with one more bounded test.
- **Evidence:** BUY-02 and BUY-03 said yes to question 6. BUY-01, BUY-02, BUY-03 and BUY-05 said the kit back accurately. BUY-01 and BUY-02 both raised quote requests that wait over the weekend before I asked about them, and BUY-01 said her FAQ isn't read. BUY-04 heard a booking machine twice. BUY-05 cannot supply written prices yet. Nobody paid, and nobody was asked to.
- **Next step and its envelope:** set the kit's prices first (chapter 4). Then send a one-page scope, built on version 2, to Meera Nair and Roland Bissonnette and ask each for a yes. Envelope: 14 days, 3 active hours, $0. Rules for that test, written now: continue if either signs or pays; stop this version if neither does after a scope call; pause if either asks for more time.
- **Rule changes for the next test (never this one):** say "no booking or lead guarantees" in the opening line of the call; ask which booking software they use.
- **What changes in the one-pager:** version 2, dated today. One change to what the kit does: quote requests that arrive in the evening or over the weekend move to the center. The kit drafts a reply to each one from the owner's facts, and the owner approves every reply before it goes. The facts file and the twenty-question FAQ stay underneath as the source of every draft, and the Monday summary stays. Version 1 listed after-hours quote requests as evidence that would change the offer, and two owners of five named them without being asked (BUY-01, BUY-02). Two changes to how the page reads: the exclusions move up to sit under the one sentence, because BUY-04 heard a booking machine twice; and "Owner supplies" adds a written price list and service area before setup starts (BUY-05) and names the person who keeps the facts current, who also runs acceptance test 2 (BUY-03). New one sentence for version 2: "For home-cleaning companies with five to fifteen staff: a reply drafted from your own facts for every evening and weekend quote request, for you to approve and send, with your top twenty customer questions and a Monday summary underneath, set up in two weeks and handed over so you can edit it." Parked in offers/later.md: a session that helps an owner write down a price list that lives in their head (BUY-05 asked; one owner is not enough to build an offer on).
