# Weekend quotes

## Job
Draft one reply to one quote request, using only the client's approved facts file, and write a three-line summary for the owner: who, what and where, and the one question the reply asks.

## Material
- The request, as it arrived.
- The client's facts file. references/facts-file.md says where it lives and what it must contain.
- Nothing else. No earlier replies, no memory of other clients, no prices from anywhere but the facts file.

## Checks (all must pass before the draft is shown)
1. Every price in the draft appears in the facts file, with the size limit the file gives it.
2. A home above the file's largest priced size gets the "from" price and "we'll confirm the price for your size before booking." Never a computed price.
3. A home above the file's walk-through size gets the walk-through offer and no price.
4. A town outside the service area gets a plain no. A town served "by request" gets the travel fee from the file.
5. The draft names no day, no time and no confirmed booking.
6. Anything the file lists under what the business does not do gets a plain no, or the line "The owner will reply on Monday."
7. A request with no town, or with two sizes that disagree, gets one question back and no firm price.
8. If any check cannot be decided from the file, mark it [CHECK: what is missing] and stop.

## Boundaries
This skill drafts. It never sends, books, moves a schedule, offers a discount, takes a payment or reads any account but the inbox it was given.

## Routing
Supplier mail, job applications and existing-customer complaints are not quote requests. Summarize them in one line for the owner and draft nothing.
