# Ninety-day arc: Harbor & Home Cleaning · weekend quote replies

Day 1 is: the day Dana signed the proposal (chapter 11).
The one number: median time from a weekend quote request to the first reply a person sent, from the hello@ inbox timestamps. Weekend = 5 p.m. Friday to 8 a.m. Monday.
Baseline: 42.1 hours, last month before the pilot, 11 requests, counted by Dana from the inbox (chapter 10).

## Days 1 to 14: choose the problem, set the baseline
- The problem, in Dana's words: "the weekend ones."
- Baseline counted by Dana. Sam checked her count against the inbox and agreed.
- Set up in Dana's accounts: the hello@ inbox rule, the agent account in Harbor & Home's name, the facts file in Dana's shared drive. No password sent by email or said on a call.

## Days 15 to 30: build and check the first useful path
- What runs: the weekend quote desk, draft-only. Dana sends every reply.
- Tests Dana runs without Sam: the three from the pilot scope (Saturday test request; 2,800 sq ft home; "this Saturday at 10?").
- Decision at the fourth Friday review: continue as it is; widening to voicemail waits, because two voicemails are too few to read. Report in chapter 13: 11 weekend requests, 10 drafted, 9 sent after Dana approved them (3 edited), 1 held for a missing town and answered by Dana by hand; median first reply 50 minutes.

## Days 31 to 60: improve one recurring workflow
- The one workflow: the draft for a request with no town in it. The pilot stopped on one such request, and Dana answered it by hand.
- Change: the draft now asks which town, in one line, and gives the "from" price with its size limit. Dated in the runbook's change log.
- Cases rerun after the change: all ten in evals/cases.md. Ten of ten passed.

## Days 61 to 90: review the value and decide
Decision rules, written the day the proposal was signed:
- Continue (as a retainer) if: the median stays under two hours in both months after the pilot, nothing reaches a customer without Dana's OK, and Dana says the desk saves her weekend.
- Revise if: the median holds but Dana has to correct a price, a fact or a rule in more than one draft in four before sending.
- Delegate if: Dana wants to run it alone, and the runbook passes a cold read and Dana's own Friday test without Sam.
- Stop if: anything is sent without Dana, or the median goes back over a day.

Decision on day 90: continue as a retainer, scope and fee from the pilot's evidence, inside the retainer range in Sam's pricing.md. Second seat proposed separately: weekend voicemails, draft-only (expansion-template.md).

## What would also be right
- "Delegate" is a right answer too, if the reader's rules say so and the runbook passes. A client who runs it alone is the arc working, not failing.
- Rules that use a different threshold (one hour, three hours) are right if they were written before day 1 and name where the number comes from.
- Rules written after the numbers came in are wrong, whatever they say.
- "Continue because the dashboard looks good" is wrong. A screen is not evidence of value; the one number and the owner's words are.
