# How I handle your data

(Fiction. Practice file for Book 5, chapter 7. Marked for attorney review before anyone reuses
it.)

- Your files stay in your accounts: your email, your drive, your AI workspace. I work in them as
  a named user you add.
- I keep no copy of your customers' details on my own machines. Tests and practice runs use
  made-up data.
- You hold every password and key. When we stop working together, you remove my user, and
  nothing of yours leaves with me.
- I keep my method, my templates and my notes on how I set things up for you, with your
  customers' details left out.
- If something goes wrong with your data, I tell you in writing the same day I find it.
