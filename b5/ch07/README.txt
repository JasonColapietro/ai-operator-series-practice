PRACTICE FILES FOR BOOK 5, CHAPTER 7: RECEIPTS SELL

Fiction. Sam Rivera, Dana Ruiz, Harbor & Home Cleaning, every competitor, every engine
answer and every date in this folder are invented for practice. "Engine 1" to "Engine 4"
stand in for real AI products on purpose, so that invented results never sit under a real
product's name. The competitors are letters for the same reason.

What is here, and which exercise uses it:

Try this: Your self-scan, written up with the losses in
  sam-scan/prompts.md          Sam's ten frozen buyer questions, dated
  sam-scan/register.csv        one row per capture, 40 rows. An id column sits in front of
                               chapter 6's eight fields so the rows sort by question.
  sam-scan/scorecard.md        the grid, with totals as fractions and coverage beside them
  sam-scan/fix-list.md         the fixes in severity order, with the dates they shipped
  agent-case-study-draft.md    a case study an AI drafted from those files. It has five
                               planted problems. Find them before you open answer-key.txt.
  sam-case-study-finished.md   one finished version. Yours will be worded differently.

Try this: A proof page with three receipts
  sam-first-page.md            Sam's first page: lots of claims, few receipts
  sam-evidence-room/           what Sam can actually show. index.md lists it, gaps.md
                               lists what Sam can't show yet.
  DISCLOSURE-sam.md            Sam's disclosure lines, used word for word
  sam-proof-page-finished.md   one finished proof page: the dated counts list first, then
                               the page source, as library/receipts/proof-page.md holds it

answer-key.txt                 the five planted problems, the claims audit of Sam's first
                               page, and what any correct proof page from these files holds

Sam's calendar in this chapter: the scan ran Tuesday, April 4, 2028. Fixes shipped April 6
and 7. The proof page went live Monday, April 10. The next reading is Tuesday, May 9, 2028.

Addresses ending in .example.com don't exist. In this practice, "checking" a receipt means
opening the file in sam-evidence-room/ that stands in for the outside record.

These files turn on no plugin, hook or setting in any tool you open them with.
