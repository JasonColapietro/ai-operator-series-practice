# My own AI visibility scan, losses included

Sam Rivera · scan run Tuesday, April 4, 2028 · published Monday, April 10, 2028

(Fiction. Practice file for Book 5, chapter 7. One finished version; yours will be worded
differently.)

## What I asked, and how

Ten questions a small cleaning or home-service owner might ask an AI assistant before hiring
someone like me: seven that name no business (discovery) and three that name me (branded). I
froze them on April 4 and asked each one once in four AI engines, in private sessions with no
history. Every answer is saved as text beside a dated screenshot. The questions and the full
register are linked below.

## Results

- Discovery: named in 0 of 26 answered. Two runs failed (one empty answer, one sign-in wall)
  and are kept out of the count.
- Branded: named in 4 of 12 answered. One of the four was wrong: it called me a certified
  bookkeeper. I'm not yet. Parts 1 and 2 of my bookkeeping certificate are complete, and the
  certificate is in progress.

## Where I lost, and to whom

Five of the seven discovery questions drew answers that named other businesses. Competitor A,
a Portland IT and AI consultancy, was named in 7 of those 15 answers. The questions on cost and
on weekend quote requests drew general advice that named nobody. One engine answered a question
about me by describing a different Sam Rivera, in another state.

## What I shipped

- April 6: the question I lost most, "Who can set up AI to answer customer questions for a
  small cleaning company in Portland, Maine?", answered in the first paragraph of my page, with
  the facts in plain text and three questions and answers below it.
- April 6: one name and one description on my page, my profile and the directory listing, with
  markup that matches.
- April 6: my certificate status, in plain text on my page.
- April 7: Harbor & Home Cleaning's About page names me as the person who keeps its books and
  set up its customer FAQ. Dana Ruiz pays me for part-time bookkeeping at Harbor & Home
  Cleaning.

## What this can't show

One run per question per engine. Answers change from week to week, and one reading of a new
practice is a starting point. I was signed out or in a fresh account, so my own history
couldn't help me.
