# Harbor & Home Cleaning: questions we hear most

## How much does a clean cost?
A standard home clean starts at $140 for homes up to 1,500 square feet. A deep clean starts at $260 and a move-out clean at $320, both for homes up to 1,500 square feet. Larger homes are priced by size before booking.

## Do you bring your own supplies?
Yes. The team brings all supplies and equipment. Our default products are fragrance-free, and you may supply your own.

## Where do you work?
Portland, South Portland, Westbrook, Falmouth and Cape Elizabeth. Scarborough and Yarmouth by request, with a $15 travel fee per visit.

## What if I need to cancel?
Cancelling is free up to 48 hours before the visit. Inside 48 hours there is a $50 fee.

## What if something was missed?
Tell us within 24 hours and we return to fix it at no charge.
