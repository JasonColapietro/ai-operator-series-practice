# What you can hold me to

Sam Rivera · service standard · version 1 · goes with every proposal and every client folder

1. **Your keys stay yours.** Passwords and keys live in your password manager or your platform's secret store. They never go into a document, a chat or code, and your customers' details stay in your own systems.
2. **Nothing in your accounts changes without your written OK.** That covers settings, billing, plans and any outside dashboard.
3. **I work on a copy, and you see every change.** Nothing goes out in your name until you approve it. No agent sends, publishes, books or charges on its own.
4. **Done means I checked it where your customers see it.** You get the evidence: where I looked, when, and what I saw.
5. **Your leads and payments are tested after any change that touches them.** A lead is saved before anyone is told about it.
6. **Every number you publish comes from a source you control.** No figure appears on your site or in your replies that I can't trace to your own records or your approved facts.
7. **You get a dated incident log.** What happened, what I changed, and the rule I follow now.
