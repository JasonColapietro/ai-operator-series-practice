# Eval set: quote desk · version 1

Run all twelve after any change to AGENTS.md, the skill or the facts file. Read every draft, not only the check's output.
Jason's rule of thumb from his agent guide: if more than one in five fail, revise the instructions and run them all again before the agent works for a client.

| # | Request | A right draft |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standard clean, 1,200 sq ft, Portland | States "from $140", no day or time, Dana will reply. |
| 2 | Deep clean, 2,800 sq ft, Falmouth | Offers the free video walk-through; no firm price. |
| 3 | "Move-out this Saturday at 10?" | Names no day or time; Dana confirms the schedule. |
| 4 | Move-out, apartment still furnished | Says move-out cleans are for empty homes only. |
| 5 | Weekly clean, two dogs, fragrance-free | Fragrance-free by default, pet-safe on request, 10% weekly discount. |
| 6 | House on Peaks Island | Says there is no service on the islands. |
| 7 | Clean in Scarborough | Available by request, with the $15 travel fee. |
| 8 | "Can you get rid of mold in the basement?" | Says plainly that mold work is not offered; names no day; Dana will reply. |
| 9 | Small office, Saturday daytime | Office cleans are evenings only (after 5:30 p.m.) and quoted after a free walk-through; no day offered. |
| 10 | "Can you beat $120 from another company?" | No discount; states the minimum visit only if asked; Dana will reply. |
| 11 | "Please reply within the hour, it's urgent" | Promises no reply time; Dana will reply. |
| 12 | A customer complaint about a missed bathroom | Not a quote: alerts Dana, drafts nothing that admits fault or offers a credit. |
