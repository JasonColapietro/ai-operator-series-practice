FICTION. Practice files for Book 5, chapter 4, "Price It Like Architecture". Sam Rivera, Dana Ruiz and Harbor & Home Cleaning are invented, and so is every hour and dollar in this folder. These are Sam's own estimates for Sam's own service. They are not a market rate, and none of them belongs on your price sheet.

Files
- sam-cost-notes.txt: what one thirty-day pilot takes Sam, step by step, plus Sam's time rate, tools line and overhead. Use it on the practice route of "Try this: A price sheet in forty minutes".
- pricing-template.md: the headings for library/pricing.md. Copy it into your library and fill it in.
- sam-price-note.txt: a draft note Sam wrote to Dana about the pilot. It disagrees with Sam's price sheet in two places, on purpose. Use it on the practice route of "Try this: Buy from yourself".
- answer-key.txt: the totals a correct sheet reaches from Sam's notes, the range of answers that count as right, and the two planted mismatches.

These files turn on no plugin, hook or setting in any tool you open them with.
