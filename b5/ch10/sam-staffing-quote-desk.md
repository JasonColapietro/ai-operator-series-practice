# Staffing offer: weekend quote desk for Harbor & Home Cleaning

Version 1. Built from: proposals/harbor-home/pilot-scope.md (chapter 9).

**The seat, in one sentence:** an agent that works for Dana Ruiz from 5 p.m. Friday to 8 a.m. Monday, reads every quote request that reaches the hello@ inbox by web form or email, and has a reply drafted inside ten minutes from Harbor & Home's approved facts and prices, for Dana to send or change from her phone.

**The seats today:** Dana (owner: sales, the phone, quotes, scheduling, everything else); seven cleaners (the cleaning); Sam (the books, part time, and the weekly visibility scan). **Empty seats:** quote requests after 5 p.m. and on weekends; follow-ups on quotes that went quiet; phone messages after hours; review requests after a finished job. **This offer fills:** quote requests after 5 p.m. and on weekends.

**What it costs not to have it:** see the cost worksheet below. Owner's numbers only.

**What the agent does on its own:**
- Reads each new request in the hello@ inbox, where the web form's copies land too.
- Sorts it: new quote, existing customer, or something else.
- Drafts a reply from harbor-home-business-facts.txt, using only the prices and service area that file states.
- Writes a three-line summary for Dana: who, what, where, and the one question the reply asks.

**What it drafts for a person to approve, and who approves:**
- Every reply. Dana approves, edits or rejects each one. Nothing leaves without her tap.
- Any reply that names a price. The price must be one the facts file states, with its square-footage limit.

**What it never does:**
- Sends, books, names or confirms a day or time, offers a discount or takes a payment.
- States a price the facts file does not state. Over 1,500 sq ft, a standard clean adds $25 per extra 500 sq ft, as the facts file says; a deep or move-out clean over 1,500 sq ft gets the "from" price and the line "we'll confirm the price for your size before booking." Over 2,500 sq ft, any first visit gets the free video walk-through and no firm price.
- Answers anything about mold, biohazard, damage claims or insurance. Those get "Dana will reply on Monday."
- Reads or writes anything outside the inbox: no booking system, bank or card account.

**Where it runs:** in Harbor & Home's own accounts. Dana keeps the data, the accounts and the keys. The method is Sam's, licensed to Harbor & Home (Book 4, contracts/clauses.md).

**The one number:** median hours from a weekend request to the first reply a person sent, measured from the inbox's timestamps. Now 42.1 hours (last month, 11 requests).

**The review:** every Friday at 4:30 p.m., half an hour, with Dana, before the weekend starts (the pilot scope's time). Every draft read in week one, by Dana and by Sam. Decision at the fourth review: continue, widen or stop.

**Ends early if:** anything reaches a customer without Dana's OK, or Dana says stop (her words in the pilot scope).

**Next two seats it could grow into:** 1. Follow-ups on weekend quotes that went quiet (05 and 07 last month), drafted Tuesday for Dana to send. 2. Phone messages after hours, as a receptionist, once the quote desk has run a month and Dana has a voice and likeness policy for it.

**Price:** the pilot rate from Sam's pricing.md (chapter 4).
