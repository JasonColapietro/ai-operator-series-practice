PRACTICE FILE, FICTION. The AI Operator Series, Book 5, Chapter 10, Sell Staffing.
Marchetti Heating & Cooling, Ellen Marchetti, her staff and every caller are invented. Westbrook and the other towns are real places near Portland, Maine. Prices are invented for practice and exist to be checked, not copied.

SAM'S NOTES FROM A CALL WITH ELLEN MARCHETTI (owner)

Business: Marchetti Heating & Cooling, Westbrook, Maine. Ellen plus four technicians and one office manager (Kim).
Office: Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. After hours, calls go to voicemail. Kim checks it at 7:30 a.m.
On call: one technician each night and weekend, by rotation. Kim keeps the rotation on a whiteboard.

What they do: furnace and boiler repair, heat pump installs, oil-to-heat-pump conversions, annual tune-ups, maintenance plans.
Prices Ellen says she'll honor (her words):
  "A diagnostic visit is $129, flat, any time in business hours."
  "A tune-up is $159."
  "Heat pump installs I quote after a site visit. Most land between $6,000 and $14,000, depends on the house."
  "Nights and weekends, a no-heat call is $249 to show up."
Service area: Westbrook, Portland, South Portland, Gorham, Windham. "Further out, I call them back."

Ellen's numbers, last month (from the voicemail log Kim keeps):
  Calls after hours: 38
  Hang-ups with no message: 14
  Messages left: 24
  Of the messages, true no-heat emergencies: 5. The on-call tech reached 3 of them that night. The other two were found at 7:30 a.m.
  Ellen: "Every hang-up in January is someone calling the next company on the list."

What Ellen wants: "Something that picks up at night, sorts the emergencies from the tune-ups, and books the tune-ups."
What worries her: "It can't tell somebody a heat pump costs six grand on the phone. And if somebody smells gas, it can't be booking them for Tuesday."
Things she always sends to a person: permits, rebate and warranty questions, insurance claims, complaints.
Recording: "We don't record now. Kim takes notes."
Languages: "Mostly English. Some French. A few Portuguese and Spanish, we get by."

Not in Sam's notes: Ellen's license number, which calendar Kim books into, and who gets called if the on-call tech doesn't answer.
