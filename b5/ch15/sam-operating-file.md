## 3. Practice rules
1. Spend less than I make, every month. Tools and overhead come out of last month's client income. Checked in the first block of the weekly review.
2. One person. Agents draft, I approve every outward step. No hire or subcontractor until the cap goes up on purpose.
3. Two offers: the weekend quote desk and the white-label check. A request outside them is referred, parked in offers/later.md, or turned down, however interesting.
4. Depth over count. Everything I leave running for a client has a runbook that passed a cold read and ten eval cases that pass, and could run a month without me.
5. My terms stay mine. No partner or backer whose needs could change my prices or terms. Client data, accounts and keys stay the client's; the method is licensed, never transferred.
