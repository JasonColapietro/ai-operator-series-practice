# Follow-ups

Two at most, about a week apart. Each one adds something the owner didn't have:

1. A second screenshot: the same question, a different engine, dated that day.
2. The one fix for their worst gap, in a line they can act on without me.

Then stop. Close the row with the date. No third message.

A "no" or a "stop", at any point: the row moves to "do not contact" the same day, for good, and the address never goes on a list.

Any other reply: answer it the same day, and offer the free check.
