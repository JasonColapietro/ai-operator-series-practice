PRACTICE FILES, FICTION. The AI Operator Series, Book 5, Chapter 8, Send the Screenshot.
Everything named in these files is invented: Sam Rivera, Nadia and her grooming shop, Curt, Joy, Martin, Theo, their businesses, their customers' questions, every engine answer and every date. Westbrook, Falmouth, South Portland, Portland and Cape Elizabeth, Maine are real places; no real business in them is described here. The engines are called Engine 1, Engine 2 and Engine 3, and the businesses they name are Competitor A and Competitor B, because the answers are invented and must not sit under a real product's or a real company's name. When you scan a real business, write the real engine names and the names exactly as the engine gave them.

Nothing in this folder sends anything. These files turn on no plugin, hook or setting in any tool you open them with.

For "Try this: Five messages from your own account"
prospects.txt            Five prospects from Sam's market list, each with the capture behind the message: the buyer's question, the engines, the date and time, and the answer as text (the text stands in for the screenshot).
template.md              The message template, the footer and the rules. Copy it to library/playbooks/outreach/template.md.
send.md                  The check before every send, and the no-retry rule. Copy it to library/playbooks/outreach/send.md and change the account names to yours.
follow-ups.md            The two-follow-up rule. Copy it to library/playbooks/outreach/follow-ups.md.
draft-check-prompt.txt   The prompt card for an agent to check your five drafts. It checks; it does not rewrite and it does not send.
messages-key.txt         Sam's five finished messages and Curt's two follow-ups, with what any correct answer must have.
sam-pipeline.md          Sam's pipeline.md, Outreach section, as of Wednesday, May 10, 2028.

Practice route: write the five messages from prospects.txt, check them with draft-check-prompt.txt, then send one to your own email address (never to a real business) so you see how it lands under your name. Log it in a practice copy of pipeline.md, outside library/, since none of these businesses exist.
