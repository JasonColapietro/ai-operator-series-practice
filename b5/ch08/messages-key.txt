Sam Rivera and every business and owner here are invented. Engine and competitor names are placeholders; see README.txt.

WHAT ANY CORRECT MESSAGE HAS
- Under seventy words above the signature (Sam's run from 46 to 49 words; yours may run longer than Sam's and still pass, as long as it stays under seventy).
- The buyer's question in quotation marks, exactly as written in prospects.txt.
- Both engines named (here, Engine 1 and Engine 2).
- Only the businesses the engine named, with nothing said about them. "They named" and "Both named" both pass. Naming the national service an engine added for prospects 2 and 4 also passes.
- "This morning", or the capture date, in the text.
- Exactly one question at the end that the owner can answer with one word. "Want the list of what would change that?", "Want the list?" and "Should I send you the three fixes?" all pass.
- The footer: full name, postal address and the stop line.
- One attachment: the dated capture.
- On the practice route, send only one, to your own email address. The four you don't send pass on everything else.

WHAT FAILS
- Two questions, or none.
- A compliment or a claim you can't show ("love your work", "you're losing customers every day").
- Anything said about a named business beyond its name ("they're paying for reviews", "a big chain").
- A price, a booking link, a report or a deck.
- The free check offered in the first message.
- Sent from a brand, shared or bot account.
