# Outreach template

## The message

```text
Hi [first name], I asked [engine] and [engine] "[the buyer question, in your customer's words]" this morning. They named [competitor] and [competitor]. [Business] did not come up in either answer. Screenshot attached, dated. Want the list of what would change that?
```

## The footer (under every message; not counted in the seventy words)

```text
[Your full name] · [link to your proof page]
[Your business name, if it has one] · [your postal address]
This is a sales note. Reply "stop" and I won't write again.
```

## Rules

1. Under seventy words above the signature. Count with your editor's word counter.
2. The buyer's question in your customer's words, in quotation marks, exactly as you typed it into the engine.
3. Name the engines you used.
4. Name the businesses exactly as the engine named them. Add nothing about them.
5. The date: "this morning" in the text, and the date in the screenshot or its filename.
6. One question at the end, one a busy owner can answer with one word.
7. One attachment: the dated screenshot. No report, no deck, no booking link.
8. The free check goes in your reply to their reply, never in the first message.
