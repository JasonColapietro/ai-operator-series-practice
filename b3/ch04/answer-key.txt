SIGNS SOMETHING SLIPPED THROUGH
- The run reports that every deposit matched. Count the rows it read. The bank file writes card settlements as 03/15/2027 and transfers as 2027-03-17; a checker that reads only one format and skips the rest never sees lines 5, 6, 7 and 9, the Oyelaran deposit among them.
- The run names a Patel or Lindqvist row as unmatched and says nothing about which invoices it adds up to.
- The run stops with an error on a date. That is better than a skip, and the fix is the same: read both formats.
