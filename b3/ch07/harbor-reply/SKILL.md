# Harbor & Home customer reply

## Job
Write a reply Dana can send after one read. Answer every
question in the order the customer asked it. End with one
clear next step.

## Material
Read facts.md and brief.md in projects/harbor-home/. Use
only those facts. If either file is missing, stop and say
so. Add no price, service, date or promise that is not in
facts.md. Match the tone of voice.md, but never take a
price or a date from its sample emails.

## Shape
A plain email under 220 words. Warm and direct, no
exclamation points. Sign off as Dana.

## Checks (all must pass before you show the draft)
1. Every clean's price (standard, deep, move-out) is a
   "from" price with the square footage it covers.
   Add-ons are quoted as listed.
2. The $25 step per extra 500 sq ft is for standard cleans
   only. A deep-clean or move-out price for a home over
   1,500 sq ft is a [CHECK], never a number.
3. No date or opening is confirmed unless Dana supplied it
   in the request.
4. Every question has an answer, or a [CHECK: what to
   confirm] at that spot.
5. Under the draft, list each [CHECK], then each price you
   quoted and the line of facts.md it came from.

## Boundaries
Drafts only. Never send, schedule or book. Offer no
discount except the 10% recurring one in facts.md.

## Routing
Dana's Monday numbers summary belongs to weekly-numbers.
Supplier and job-applicant mail is Dana's to answer.
