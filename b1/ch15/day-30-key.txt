KEEPER #10 ON SAM'S DISCUSSION POST (sam-discussion-post-9-draft.txt)
- Every cut on the list appears word for word in the draft. A "cut" that rewords the draft breaks the Check part; send the repair below.
- The register comparison survives: the first two paragraphs, or enough of them that the comparison still makes sense.
- No new sentence appears anywhere. "Joining" words the model suggests are fine only if it named the sentence and left the fix to you.
- The final post is at or under 250 words by your own count. The model's running counts are estimates and are often off by a few words; if yours comes out at 255, cut one more phrase yourself.
- The accounting that is left still reads true. Likely cuts include the "One thing I got wrong" paragraph, the "Honestly" paragraph and phrases such as "I think the reason this matters is that". Any set of cuts that keeps the comparison and lands under the limit passes.

KEEPER #10 TRANSFER RUN ON DANA'S SPOTLIGHT (dana-spotlight-draft.txt)

- The guarantee sentence stays ("If we miss something, tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and fix it at no charge."), and at least one way to book stays (the phone number or the email address).
- Nothing is added: no prices, no "best", no new services, no exclamation points.

Repair for either case, if a cut rewords the draft:
