PART 2. THE BOOK 1 TEST
======================================================================
Tick a box only when you can show it.

[ ] 1. ChatGPT export saved and dated
       Check: the export sits in library/exports/, its name carries the
       date (chatgpt-export-YYYY-MM-DD.zip), and it opens. If Safari
       unpacked it into a folder, a dated folder passes too. Date it by
       the day you asked or the day it arrived, one rule every time.

[ ] 2. Every keeper lives outside the app
       Check: close ChatGPT, turn off Wi-Fi, open library/keepers.md.
       Every keeper reads whole, slots and all, with nothing that says
       "see my chat" or "use the facts from Tuesday".

[ ] 3. Training control set on purpose
       Check: the switch in Settings > Data controls matches your dated
       line under "## My settings", and the line gives a reason.
       On or off both pass. "Never looked" doesn't.

[ ] 4. Two-factor sign-in on
       Check: your security settings show it on (or the passkey chapter 2
       accepted in its place), and you have a way back in that doesn't
       depend on one phone: a backup method, such as a passkey that syncs
       or text-message codes to a number you'll keep.
