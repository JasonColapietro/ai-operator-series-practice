PART 3. BLANK TWO-DAY SCORECARD (the same table Keeper #8 holds: fill
it in here while you run, then copy the marks into the keeper)
Marks: Named · Not named · Wrong · Failed run
  Named       the business is there, and right as far as you can tell
  Not named   a real answer that leaves the business out (it names others,
              or says it knows nothing about the business)
  Wrong       the business is there, with a wrong fact or a mix-up
  Failed run  no real answer (an error, a blank, a reply cut off). Ask again
              in a new Temporary Chat, and mark Failed run only if it fails
              twice.
Under Also named, write every other business the answer named. If it gave
general advice and named no business at all, mark Not named and write
"nobody" there. On question 4, write which of the two it would pick.
