PRACTICE FILES, FICTION. The AI Operator Series, Book 1, Chapter 13: Ask About Yourself.
The worked example in these files uses Harbor & Home Cleaning and Dana Ruiz, who are invented.

This chapter's exercise runs on a REAL business: your own, your employer's (ask first), or a local
favorite. ChatGPT has never heard of Harbor & Home, because it doesn't exist, so Dana's questions
are here to show the wording only. Don't run them; they would only tell you what the chat makes of
a name nobody has written about.

buyer-questions-worksheet.txt   The five kinds of buyer question as fill-ins, Dana's wording as a
                                worked example, and a blank two-day scorecard (the same table
                                Keeper #8 holds in library/keepers.md). Copy the worksheet into
                                library/scans/[BUSINESS]/ and fill it in there.
scorecard-two-day.csv           The same scorecard as a spreadsheet file. It opens in Excel,
                                Numbers or Google Sheets. One row per question, one column per day.

Never run these questions on a private person: a neighbor, a coworker, a date, an ex. Asking about
yourself is fine. Asking about a business is the point.

Nothing here turns on a plugin, hook or setting in any tool you open it with.
