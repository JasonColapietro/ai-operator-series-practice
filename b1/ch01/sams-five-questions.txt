SAM'S FIVE QUESTIONS FOR THE WEEK OF NOVEMBER 2, 2026
Practice file for Book 1 (First Login), chapter 1, The Blank Box.

FICTION. Sam Rivera, the store, the bookkeeping class, the instructor and
everyone else named here are invented for practice. Any resemblance to real
people or places is a coincidence. Built from Sam's notes for the same week
(the weekly-plan files that chapter 5 uses), with no new facts added.

How to use this file: start a new chat for each question, paste the question

1. THE THURSDAY SHIFT

I work retail and take an evening class on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 6:30 to 9. This week's posted schedule has me working Thursday from 4 to 10, and when I asked my manager about it on Friday she said she'd see what she could do, but I haven't heard back. What are my options, and what should I say to her first?

2. THE EMAIL TO MY INSTRUCTOR

My evening bookkeeping class asks us to email the instructor before missing a class, and late work loses 10 percent. If my Thursday shift stands, I'll miss the class where a practice set is due at 6:30. How should I ask my instructor, in a short email, whether sending the practice set by email before class counts as on time?

3. THE QUIZ REVIEW

I have a quiz on Tuesday, November 10, on chapters 5, 6 and 7 of an intro bookkeeping course, and I want about three hours of review before it, spread over several days instead of crammed into the Monday night before. How should I split those three hours, and what should each session cover?

4. THE DISCUSSION POST

For my evening class I have to write a 250-word discussion post by Sunday at 11:59 p.m., and I've set aside about 45 minutes for it. How should I use those 45 minutes to finish a post that lands close to 250 words?

5. THE FREE EVENING

This week I work Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday, I have class Tuesday and Thursday nights, and I'm at my mom's for lunch on Sunday. I still need about two hours for laundry and groceries before next Monday, and I want one evening with nothing on it. Which evening should I protect, and where do the errands fit?
