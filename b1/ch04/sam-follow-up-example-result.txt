Things to sort out before you send
1. Denise's last name and the store's name. Your notes don't include either, and Janelle may want both when she calls. Add them if Denise is fine with that.
2. Your January to April availability. In the interview you said "probably more hours" but weren't sure. Your notes say Part 2 of your certificate is Tuesday and Thursday evenings, so it doesn't clash with a daytime job. Decide how many hours you could offer before she asks again. I left it out of the email so you don't commit to something by accident.
3. The store number has no area code in your notes. Check whether Janelle would need one to call it.
4. You don't know whether they've chosen someone else. The email doesn't guess. If she replies that the role is filled, a short thank-you keeps the door open for busy season.
